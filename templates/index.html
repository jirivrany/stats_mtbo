Menu of the day

  • At the 16th European MTBO Championship in Almeida Portugal, Hanes Hnilica has become the first athlete to win all posible gold medals in one championship — in the sprint, middle, mass start and mixed relay. During his impresive yet short carrer only these distances were held at EMTBOC so he also completed the EMTBOC Grand Slam as only the second athlete in history, after Erik Knudsen Skovgaard
  • Warsaw hosted the 22nd World MTBO Championship in 2025. Krystof Bogar became the most successful athlete in WMTBOC history during his 10th championship.
    On the same championship, Martina Stepankova (Tichovska) completed the WMTBOC Grand Slam, becoming the first athlete in history to win all possible gold medals on WMTBOC distances held during her career.
  • At the 15th European MTBO Championship in Vilnius, Martina Stepankova (Tichovska) became the most successful athlete in EMTBOC history, ahead of Krystof Bogar and Vojtech Ludvik — her teammates from the victorious mixed relay team.
