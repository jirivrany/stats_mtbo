The best single championships ever - who collected most gold medals in one year. Unlike the Grand Slam, which looks at a whole career, every row here is a single year. At least {{ min_gold }} gold medals in that year are required to be listed - a single title is a fine result, but a long way from a perfect championship.

Ranked the olympic way: gold decides first, silver breaks a tie, then bronze. Three golds and a silver therefore beat three golds and two bronzes. The Races column shows how many races of that kind were held that year - the individual table does not count the relay. Among rows with the same medals, winning every race held that year (🏆) comes first: there was simply nothing more to win.
