Disclaimer:

Before 2015 were the relay results usually published with names only (without the IOF ID). Therefore all the name/id pairing of old results was done manually. Please report any mistakes you found, I will correct them gladly.

The IOF rule 24.10 says "If a Federation is represented by two or more teams in a relay class, only the team with the better result is considered in determining the placings." Those second and further teams are listed separately, without a place - they did take part, so they count towards participation statistics.

Teams with riders from more than one Federation start non-competitively (rule 6.8), so they are not part of the official results at all and are not listed here.
